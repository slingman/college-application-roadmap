🖥️ APPLICATION PLATFORMS

🟢 Common AppOne application can serve hundreds of participating colleges. Usually includes a personal statement plus school-specific supplements.
🔵 UC ApplicationOne application for all 9 UC campuses. Includes 4 of 8 Personal Insight Questions, activities/awards and academic history. No recommendations required.
🟠 CSU ApplicationOne CSU application for participating campuses. Requirements and supplemental materials vary; check campus/program requirements.
🟣 Coalition / School-SpecificSome colleges use Coalition or their own application. Verify the platform, testing policy, recommendations, essays and supplemental essays.

Do not assume a school follows the "typical" Common App process. Examples of schools that can have special requirements include MIT, Georgetown and some programs at other universities.
